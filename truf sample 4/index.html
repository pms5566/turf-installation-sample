Our Workflow

Step-by-Step Turf Installation Process

We follow a rigorous landscaping prep method to ensure your synthetic turf never wrinkles, pools, or separates at the seams.

Step 1

Survey & Measurement

We map out the exact lawn dimensions, evaluate drainage runoff directions, and clear away weeds or existing sod base.

Step 2

Excavation & Setting

We dig 2–3 inches down, lay crushed stone base, roll out weed barriers, and compact the sub-base firmly.

Step 3

Final Inspection & Infill

We lay turf, glue seam junctions invisibly, spread pet infill, and brush fibers upright with a power groomer.

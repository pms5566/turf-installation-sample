FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about drainage, heat, or maintenance? We have answers.

How does water and pet waste drain through the turf?
Our premium turf uses a fully permeable double-coated backing that drains liquid at over 30 inches per hour. Pet urine flows straight through into the underlying crushed stone base. Solid waste is scooped up as usual, and the area can be rinsed with a hose. ZeoFill infill neutralizes odor-causing microbes to keep the yard hygienic.
Does the turf get hot in direct Texas summer sun?
Synthetic turf can get warm in direct midday summer sun. To prevent overheating, we utilize specialized light-reflective infills (CoolSand/Silica) and heat-resistant polymers that lower surface temperatures by up to 25% compared to standard builder-grade turf. A quick spray of water cools the grass down instantly if needed.
How long does artificial turf installation take?
Most residential projects (under 2,000 sq ft) are completed in 1 to 2 days. Larger estates, commercial properties, or complex putting greens with significant elevation contours and bunkers may take 3 to 4 days.
What kind of warranty do you provide?
All our turf comes with an industry-leading 15-year manufacturer warranty against fading, color fading, fiber degradation, and drainage defects. Our custom base prep work is also guaranteed to resist settling and shifts.
